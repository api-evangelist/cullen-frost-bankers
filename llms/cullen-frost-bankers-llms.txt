## What an agent can and cannot do here

- There is **no callable Frost Bank API**. Do not construct requests against `api.frostbank.com`; that
  host is Imperva-fronted, returns HTTP 403 at the root and 404 on every discovery path, and serves the
  bank's own online/mobile banking clients rather than third-party developers.
- Consumer account **data sharing is delegated to Plaid**. Frost announced a partnership with Plaid on
  2022-02-28 using Plaid Exchange, so the machine-readable surface a third party integrates against is
  Plaid's, not Frost's. Integrate with Plaid and use Frost as the institution.
- **Commercial and treasury integration** (ACH origination, wires, file delivery, reporting) runs
  through the Frost Connect and Frost Treasury Connect portals under a signed customer agreement. There
  is no self-service onboarding and no public contract.
